## 🎨 **Visual States Summary**

| State | Visual | Actions Available |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| **OFF (First Time)** | ⚪ OFF [Enable →] | Enable (shows inline config) |
| **Enabling** | Inline config form | Select clusters, strategy, Turn On |
| **ON (Active)** | 🟢 ON [⚙️ Settings] [⏸️ Pause] | Settings, Pause, View dashboard |
| **PAUSED** | ⏸️ PAUSED [▶️ Resume] | Resume, Settings |
| **OFF (Disabled)** | ⚪ OFF [Enable →] | Re-enable (remembers previous config) |

---

## 🔧 **Key Features**

✅ **No wizard** - inline config appears when clicking "Enable"  
✅ **Quick toggle** - ON/OFF/Pause with single click  
✅ **Mode switch** - Radio button to switch Manual ↔ Karpenter view  
✅ **Per-cluster control** - Enable/disable per cluster in Settings  
✅ **Smart defaults** - "Balanced" strategy pre-selected  
✅ **Persistent config** - Remembers settings when re-enabling  
✅ **Clear status** - Always shows what's happening  
✅ **Mobile friendly** - Collapses nicely on small screens

## 🎯 **User Flow Examples**

### **First Time Enable**
1. Click **[Enable →]**
2. Select 2 clusters → Auto-calculates savings
3. Keep "Balanced" strategy (pre-selected)
4. Click **[Turn On Karpenter]**
5. Done ✅ (30 seconds total)

### **Pause During Incident**
1. Click **[⏸️ Pause]**
2. Confirm
3. Done ✅ (5 seconds)

### **Resume After Testing**
1. Click **[▶️ Resume]**
2. Done ✅ (1 click)

### **Change Strategy**
1. Click **[⚙️ Settings]**
2. Change dropdown: Balanced → Cost-First
3. Click **[Save]**
4. Done ✅ (10 seconds)

### **Turn Off Completely**
1. Click **[⚙️ Settings]**
2. Scroll to bottom → **[Turn Off Karpenter]**
3. Confirm
4. Done ✅ (15 seconds)
